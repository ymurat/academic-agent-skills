### What it does

Answers EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) questions: risk classification, Art. 5 bans, Annex III high-risk duties, GPAI, Art. 50 transparency.

### When to use

Load this skill when the task matches: eU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) questions: risk classification, Art. 5 bans, Annex III high-risk duties, GPAI and Code of Practice, Art. 50 transparency, timelines, penalties, mapping to ISO 42001 and NIST.

### Example prompts

- Classify this student grading LLM under the EU AI Act risk tiers. Cite articles.
- What Annex III duties apply if we deploy this high-risk hiring screen?
