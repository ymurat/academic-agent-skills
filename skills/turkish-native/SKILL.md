# Natural Turkish

Upstream: `resources/turkish-native/` (oguzhankayan/turkish-native, MIT). Read its SKILL.md in full, then only the references needed. Ignore `scripts/validate-*.py`, which are developer validators.

1. The upstream is written for marketing, UI and legal copy. For academic text, keep its diagnosis of translationese but follow Turkish academic conventions: precise terms, a formal register, and technical terms kept in English when no established Turkish equivalent exists.
2. Follow TDK spelling. Use the passive or impersonal voice only when the discipline expects it.
3. When the user shares text that sounds artificial, explain briefly why, then give a natural rewrite.
4. Don't change meaning, data or citations. Flag any sentence whose meaning is unclear instead of guessing.
5. No em dashes and no buzzwords; vary sentence length.
