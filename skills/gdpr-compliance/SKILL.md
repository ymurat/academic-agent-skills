# GDPR compliance

Upstream skill (Sushegaad GRC repo, MIT, vetted low risk on 2026-10-31, markdown only): `resources/gdpr-compliance/`.

1. Read `skills/gdpr-compliance/SKILL.md` in full, then the references it names (`documents.md`, `dpa-template.md`, `privacy-notice.md`, `updates-2025.md`).
2. If data subjects, controllers or processing are in Turkey, also load the kvkk skill and apply both; state where they differ.
3. Before quoting an article number, deadline, fine or EDPB guideline, confirm it against the official text on EUR-Lex or edpb.europa.eu and give the date checked. The upstream `updates-2025.md` may be outdated.
4. For AI systems, cross-refer eu-ai-act; for research data, ethics-committee.
5. This is guidance, not legal advice; say so when the stakes are high.
6. Keep any real personal data on local storage.
