## English

### What it does

Audits systems or data flows for GDPR and drafts privacy notices, DPAs or consent text. Pair with kvkk for Turkey.

### When to use

Load this skill when the task matches: auditing systems or data flows for GDPR, drafting privacy notices, DPAs or consent text, or answering GDPR questions; pair with kvkk for Turkey.

### Example prompts

- Audit this survey data flow for GDPR. Draft a privacy notice.
- Draft a DPA outline for our EU university partner processing thesis interview audio.
