# UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of AI (2021). From the UNESCO page, 2026-11-01.
Four core values: (1) respecting and promoting human dignity and human rights; (2) fostering just, peaceful, and interconnected societies; (3) ensuring diversity and inclusiveness; (4) supporting flourishing of environment and ecosystems (wording of the UNESCO web page).
Ten principles: proportionality and do no harm; safety and security; right to privacy and data protection; multi-stakeholder and adaptive governance and collaboration; responsibility and accountability; transparency and explainability; human oversight and determination; sustainability; awareness and literacy; fairness and non-discrimination.
Policy action areas exist (e.g. gender, data, international cooperation, ethical impact assessment); the exact list and paragraph numbers were NOT extracted, read the full text (UNESDOC, SHS/BIO/PI/2021/1) before citing paragraphs. Related tools: Readiness Assessment Methodology (RAM), Ethical Impact Assessment (EIA).
