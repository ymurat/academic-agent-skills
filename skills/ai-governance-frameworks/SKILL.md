# AI governance frameworks (international soft and hard law)

Covers the three cross-border instruments that `eu-ai-act`, `nist-ai-rmf` and `iso42001` do not: OECD AI Principles, UNESCO Recommendation, Council of Europe Framework Convention. Use those three skills for their own articles and clauses.

## Nature of each instrument
- OECD Recommendation (OECD/LEGAL/0449): non-binding Council recommendation; adopted 2019, definition revised 2023, text revised 3 May 2024. Five values-based principles (1.1 to 1.5) and five policy recommendations (2.1 to 2.5). Its AI system definition is reused in other frameworks.
- UNESCO Recommendation (2021): non-binding, adopted by 193 Member States; four values, ten principles, policy action areas; tools such as the Readiness Assessment Methodology and Ethical Impact Assessment.
- CETS 225 (Vilnius, 5 September 2024): binding treaty for the Parties that ratify it; principles in Articles 6 to 13, remedies in 14 and 15, risk and impact management in 16. Check signature and ratification status, including Türkiye's, live on the Council of Europe treaty office page; it is not verified here.

## Workflow
1. **Purpose.** Compliance gap analysis, policy brief, course module, or paper section. Identify jurisdiction (Türkiye, EU market, public sector) and which instruments actually apply versus are used as benchmarks.
2. **Inventory the system or policy.** Lifecycle phase (use the OECD lifecycle terms), actors, affected people, rights at stake.
3. **Map by theme** using `crosswalk.md`: for each theme record the requirement, the instrument and its exact provision (quote from the saved texts), current practice, gap, action.
4. **Bring in the detailed frameworks.** Hand legal duties to `eu-ai-act`, risk processes to `nist-ai-rmf`, management system controls to `iso42001`, personal data to `kvkk` and `gdpr-compliance`, security to `ai-threat-review`.
5. **Report.** Table plus short narrative; every provision cited as instrument, provision number and URL with access date. For Turkish texts, use `turkish-native`; use a Turkish title for an instrument only if an official or published Turkish translation uses it.

## Metaverse and XR
None of the three instruments targets XR by name. Map XR issues (biometric and behavioural data, manipulation, minors, identity in virtual spaces) to the themes above and state that this is an interpretation.

## Rules
- Never cite an article, paragraph or principle number that is not in the saved texts. UNESCO paragraph numbers are not saved yet: open the full text first.
- Distinguish binding from non-binding; do not call OECD or UNESCO texts "law".
- The G7 Hiroshima code of conduct is optional; open its primary text before using it.
