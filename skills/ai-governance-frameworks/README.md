# ai-governance-frameworks

[AI governance frameworks (international soft and hard law)](./SKILL.md)

## English

### What it does

Maps an AI system, policy, course or paper to the OECD AI Principles (2024), UNESCO Recommendation (2021) or Council of Europe Framework Convention.

### When to use

Load this skill when the task matches: the user maps an AI system, policy, course or paper to the OECD AI Principles (2024), the UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of AI (2021) or the Council of Europe Framework Convention on AI (CETS 225), or compares them with the EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF and ISO/IEC 42001.

### Example prompts

- Map this campus AI chatbot policy to the OECD AI Principles (2024).
- Which UNESCO Recommendation articles apply to this student-facing LLM tool?

### Inputs and outputs

**Inputs**

- User task and relevant documents
- Constraints stated by the user

**Outputs**

- Structured output defined in SKILL.md
- Limitations and unverifiable items marked plainly

### Limitations

- Never cite an article, paragraph or principle number that is not in the saved texts.
- Never cite an article, paragraph or principle number that is not in the saved texts. UNESCO paragraph numbers are not saved yet: open the full text first.
- Distinguish binding from non-binding; do not call OECD or UNESCO texts "law".
