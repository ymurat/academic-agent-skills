## SPI Manifesto principles (short headers, from PDF)
People: know the culture and focus on needs; motivate all people involved; base improvement on experience and measurements; create a learning organisation.
Business: support the organisation's vision and objectives; use dynamic and adaptable models as needed; apply risk management.
Change: manage the organisational change in your improvement effort; ensure all parties understand and agree on process; do not lose focus.
