### What it does

Formats a Turkish manuscript for TR Dizin or DergiPark and lints Turkish APA 7 references (ve ark., aktaran, t.y., bilingual öz/abstract).

- Check öz/abstract bilingual consistency and TR Dizin first-page statements.
