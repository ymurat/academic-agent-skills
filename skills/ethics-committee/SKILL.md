# Ethics committee review

Full upstream skill (Marazii/research-co-pilot, `ethics-committee`): `resources/ethics-committee/SKILL.md`.

1. Read the upstream SKILL.md in full and follow its review: consent, risk and benefit, vulnerable groups, privacy, deception, payment, dual use, and AI or LLM use in research. End with a decision letter listing required and recommended revisions.
2. Ignore its references to skills and commands that aren't installed here (methodology-advisor, replication-designer, /methodology, /ethics).
3. The upstream is written around US IRBs. For Turkish studies, also assess against the university's ethics board (Etik Kurul) requirements, KVKK (Law No. 6698) alongside GDPR, and TÜBİTAK's research and publication ethics rules. Check the current versions on official sites and give the date checked.
4. Never invent board rules or legal provisions. If the user's institution-specific requirements are unknown, say so and ask for the board's forms or guidelines.
5. Keep protocols and participant data on local storage and out of outside services.
6. Follow these writing rules: formal, precise, weaknesses first, no em dashes, no buzzwords.
